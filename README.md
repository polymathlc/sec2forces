# Forces & Energy Lab

Interactive Lower Secondary Science lesson for **Chapter 9: Applications of Forces and Transfer of Energy**, built for Polymath Learning Centre.

The repo holds two single-file apps. Open either in a browser or serve them with any static host.

| Page | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | The full Chapter 9 textbook lesson (sections 9.1 to 9.5, worked examples, formula sheet) |
| `test-paper-lab.html` | Interactive notes built from the Unit 9 test paper (26 Oct 2026): force, gravity, magnets, friction, pressure, work done and energy |

## What's inside `index.html`

| Section | Interactive tools |
| --- | --- |
| 9.1 How do forces in nature affect us? | Wave-breaker coastline (coral reef and mangrove protection), hazard simulator (earthquake, tsunami, volcano, tropical cyclone, climate) |
| 9.2 What are the types of forces? | Four forces on a plane, contact vs non-contact sorter, force gallery (normal, tension, friction, elastic, gravitational, magnetic, electric), spring load, free-body diagram builder, mass vs weight on four worlds |
| 9.3 What happens when objects interact? | Effects-of-force deck, cyclist (balanced and unbalanced forces), F = ma, door and moments beam, brick pressure, mountain air pressure, straw and suction cup, water jets, liquid columns, depth pressure (submarine, dam, container shapes) |
| 9.4 What are energy and work? | Energy forms explorer, GPE/KE/EPE/electrical calculators, energy pendulum, playground slide, conversion detective, drop and bounce, work or no work, push the crate, shelves, activity power, stair power |
| 9.5 What are the sources of energy? | Renewable sorter, power station explorer, nuclear chain reaction with control rods, fusion |
| Practice | All 18 worked examples from the chapter as try-it cards with answer checking and step-by-step working, plus a formula sheet |

The print button appears when the page is opened directly (not when embedded) and prints on A4.

## What's inside `test-paper-lab.html`

| Part | Interactive tools |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Force | Five effects of a force (start, stop, change direction, change speed, change shape), stretch or squash a spring, contact vs non-contact sorter, spring balance with a non-zero starting reading |
| 2 Gravity | Throw a ball up (gravity on and off), same mass on five worlds, weight formula triangle, two masses attracting, satellite orbit |
| 3 Magnets | Two bar magnets (flip, release, gap), test eight objects with a magnet, applications flip cards |
| 4 Friction | Push a block across five surfaces with wheels and grease, useful-or-nuisance cards, ball falling from a 100 m building with F₁ and F₂ and a speed–time graph |
| 5 Pressure | Shoes on soft sand, flip the 8 cm × 6 cm × 4 cm block, cm² to m² grid, pressure formula triangle |
| 6 Work done | Push the crate, five "is any work done?" scenarios, apples onto a shelf, work formula triangle |
| 7 Energy | Seven energy sources with energy-change chains, 500 kg roller coaster with friction, energy flow through five appliances |
| Recap | Formula cards, units table, common traps, two opposing forces on one block |

It has the same A4 print button. Staff signed in as the admin can add images to each part (Firebase), and the version badge shows only for the admin.
